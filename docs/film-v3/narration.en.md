# Narration draft (en)

## 0-11s: Human potential

A question begins at home. Why has the water around us changed? Curiosity is a beginning. Understanding needs opportunities to learn, and people who listen.

## 11-22s: The changing world

Digital tools connect more of our world. But access to technology is not the same as capability. We must understand its limits, and remain responsible for our decisions.

## 22-35s: Education

Families, teachers and universities help us ask better questions, compare evidence and develop judgement. Learning becomes useful when we can explain not only an answer, but why we trust it.

## 35-47s: Knowledge into application

Practice connects knowledge with real needs. Teams may explore monitoring, data and infrastructure. Their work must be tested, documented and connected to appropriate professional expertise.

## 47-61s: The wider system

A useful solution needs more than technology. Institutions must agree who operates it, maintains it and reviews the information. Governance connects authority with safeguards and accountability. Communities must be heard.

## 61-73s: Independent capability

Independent capability grows when local people can adapt, maintain and teach what they use. Experience returns to education and public decisions, revealing what works and what still needs to change.

## 73-84s: The complete world

Smart World Education connects these steps: education, understanding, practical application and independent capability. Explore how one person's learning becomes part of a whole society's development.
